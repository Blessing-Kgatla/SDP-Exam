# UI/UX design briefs for the 40 product categories

Use these as **design briefs**, not identical page templates. Each entry defines the visual character, recommended primary screens/interactions, and clichés to avoid.

### A. Commerce and service platforms

#### 1. E-commerce — editorial retail
- **Character:** Curated shop, thoughtful physical retail, natural colors, strong product photography, brand-appropriate typography.
- **Homepage:** Campaign photograph/visual, clear categories, seasonal collections, carefully selected merchandise.
- **Catalog:** Search, filtering, sorting, product image, price, size/color/availability; consistent product grids.
- **Product:** Large image gallery, variants, price, description, shipping/returns, trust details, add to cart.
- **Checkout:** Minimal distractions, transparent totals, shipping and payment steps, visible order summary.
- **Mobile:** Simple category navigation, reachable filters and purchase control.
- **Distinctive:** Editorial merchandising and subtle motion.
- **Avoid:** Floating fake product cards, generic glowing hero, meaningless statistics.

#### 2. Multi-vendor marketplace — discovery and comparison
- **Character:** Functional, impartial, trustworthy, information-rich.
- **Primary screens:** Search-dominant homepage, category exploration, comparable listings, seller profiles, listing details, cart/checkout.
- **Special UX:** Seller reputation, price/delivery/condition comparisons, buyer protection and disputes.
- **Distinctive:** Side-by-side comparison panel; useful list/map/filter views if relevant.
- **Avoid:** Marketing spectacle blocking search; sellers indistinguishable from one another.

#### 3. Restaurant and food ordering — culinary and warm
- **Character:** Authentic food, atmosphere and heritage; cream, terracotta, green, charcoal or brand-derived colors.
- **Primary screens:** Restaurant story, menu organized by course/category, ordering/reservations, location/hours, checkout.
- **Special UX:** Legible item descriptions/prices, allergy/dietary info, pickup/delivery or table availability where relevant.
- **Distinctive:** Menu-inspired typography, genuine photographs, calm transitions.
- **Avoid:** Food emoji as substitutes for imagery, buried menus, excessive glossy cards.

#### 4. Travel and tourism — destination storytelling
- **Character:** Exploratory and photography-led with geographical accents.
- **Primary screens:** Destination/date/traveler search, curated destinations, itinerary builder, maps, booking details.
- **Special UX:** Route/day timelines, local context, transport and availability clarity.
- **Distinctive:** Location-led storytelling and contextual maps.
- **Avoid:** Identical destination cards, invented details, search that is only decorative.

#### 5. Hotel booking — calm hospitality
- **Character:** Serene, elegant, inviting and operationally clear.
- **Primary screens:** Property search, availability, room comparison, amenities, property gallery, booking and confirmation.
- **Special UX:** Total prices and fees, occupancy, cancellation rules, check-in details.
- **Distinctive:** Floor plans and amenity comparisons.
- **Avoid:** Decorative images hiding the booking path or actual pricing.

#### 6. Real estate — architectural and spatial
- **Character:** Architectural photography, editorial typography, precise grids.
- **Primary screens:** Split map/list property search, listing filters, property gallery, floor plans, agent contact, viewing requests.
- **Special UX:** Price, location, area, beds/baths, neighborhood context and comparison.
- **Distinctive:** Spatial exploration and property/plan viewers.
- **Avoid:** Tiny generic cards that obscure photography and important details.

#### 7. Events and ticketing — poster-inspired
- **Character:** Expressive typography, event artwork, energetic event-appropriate palettes.
- **Primary screens:** Upcoming events, genre/date filters, event page, venue/seat selector, tickets and check-in.
- **Special UX:** Dates, location, prices, ticket tiers and accessibility information easy to scan.
- **Distinctive:** Poster-like event composition and schedule/calendar discovery.
- **Avoid:** Every event looking identical; unclear ticket quantities or fees.

#### 8. Professional service booking — trust and simplicity
- **Character:** Friendly, genuine, professional.
- **Primary screens:** Services and prices, provider profiles, availability calendar, scheduling, confirmation, rescheduling.
- **Special UX:** Duration, location, cancellation policy, confirmation and reminders.
- **Distinctive:** Step-by-step booking with transparent details.
- **Avoid:** Long marketing journey before users can find availability.

### B. Business, SaaS and enterprise

#### 9. Corporate/company website — institutional confidence
- **Character:** Authentic, authoritative, restrained, carefully art-directed.
- **Primary screens:** Purposeful homepage, services, real projects/case studies, leadership, accomplishments, contact.
- **Distinctive:** Project photography, clear timelines, custom business diagrams.
- **Avoid:** Gradient hero → three feature cards → fake stats → fake testimonials → CTA.

#### 10. SaaS platform — product-led precision
- **Character:** Modern, direct, credible; marketing design separated from functional application design.
- **Marketing:** Real annotated product screenshots, workflows, integrations, benefits, pricing and demo.
- **In-app:** Efficient product-specific navigation and actionable work area.
- **Distinctive:** Authentic interactive walkthroughs and product demos.
- **Avoid:** Floating imaginary dashboards, artificial glowing orbs, vague claims.

#### 11. ERP/admin dashboard — dense and systematic
- **Character:** Information-first, neutral panels, status colors and compact typography.
- **Primary screens:** Sidebar navigation, advanced tables, filters, saved views, record details, bulk actions, reports, audit logs.
- **Distinctive:** Keyboard operations and customizable views.
- **Avoid:** Large cards for every database record and hidden actions.

#### 12. Project management — organized workspace
- **Character:** Focused work surface, subdued colors, purpose-specific status accents.
- **Primary screens:** Projects, tasks, list/board/calendar/timeline views, task detail side panel, files/comments, members.
- **Distinctive:** Several views of the same data, contextual edits, fluid drag-and-drop.
- **Avoid:** Decorative homepage dashboards slowing down task work.

#### 13. CRM and sales — relationship-centered
- **Character:** Business-readable, compact, context-rich.
- **Primary screens:** Contact and company lists, pipeline, customer profile, communication history, activities, reports.
- **Distinctive:** One customer timeline connecting deals, messages, tasks and documents.
- **Avoid:** Disconnected cards and redundant metrics.

#### 14. Banking/fintech — financial clarity
- **Character:** Secure, precise, legible, trustworthy, restrained.
- **Primary screens:** Balance, transactions, transfers, savings, statements, settings and security.
- **Special UX:** Clear money movement, fees, timestamps, confirmation and irreversible-action safeguards.
- **Distinctive:** Accurate numeric typography, understandable financial charts.
- **Avoid:** Decorative dashboards, misleading charts, ambiguous financial actions.

#### 15. Recruitment — jobs-first search
- **Character:** Professional, accessible, comparable listings.
- **Primary screens:** Job search with filters and list/detail split view, role pages, employer profiles, applications, tracking.
- **Distinctive:** Salary and application progress clarity.
- **Avoid:** Large marketing tiles that prevent efficient vacancy comparison.

#### 16. Developer tools — developer-native
- **Character:** Technical, precise, powerful yet minimal.
- **Primary screens:** Console/workspace, API docs, code examples, logs, errors, settings and usage.
- **Distinctive:** Command palette, keyboard shortcuts, copyable code, syntax and API explorer.
- **Avoid:** Decorations that harm code readability or debugging.

### C. Media, entertainment and creativity

#### 17. News/journalism — editorial hierarchy
- **Character:** Publisher masthead, authoritative typography, meaningful story hierarchy.
- **Primary screens:** Feature story, section indexes, story articles, author pages, topic discovery and search.
- **Distinctive:** Multi-column magazine compositions; article priority affects design.
- **Avoid:** Identical rounded cards for every news item.

#### 18. Video/music streaming — immersive discovery
- **Character:** Media artwork first, dark/adaptive surfaces, quiet controls.
- **Primary screens:** Featured content, personalized shelves, search, player, playlists/watchlists, history.
- **Distinctive:** Artwork-led presentation and focused transition to playback.
- **Avoid:** Dashboard-like statistics or competing chrome around media.

#### 19. Gaming/esports — genre-specific expression
- **Character:** Based on the game's actual world: a chess game should not look like a shooter or cozy puzzle game.
- **Primary screens:** Main menu, game modes, characters/teams, progression, leaderboards, settings, gameplay HUD.
- **Distinctive:** Custom typography, game-world motifs, deliberate animation.
- **Avoid:** Default cyberpunk neon for every game and unreadable UI.

#### 20. Creative portfolio/agency — art-directed work
- **Character:** Distinctive typography, purposeful composition, visually led projects.
- **Primary screens:** Featured work, project case studies, process, about, contact.
- **Distinctive:** Custom project transitions and case-study narratives.
- **Avoid:** Generic SaaS cards, invented reviews, gimmicky motion.

### D. Education and knowledge

#### 21. Learning management system — learner progress
- **Character:** Clear, supportive, scholarly and structured.
- **Primary screens:** Current courses, upcoming assessments, lesson/module views, submissions, feedback, grades, progress.
- **Distinctive:** Learning paths with visible next steps and progress.
- **Avoid:** Motivational decoration displacing academic work.

#### 22. School/university website — academic identity
- **Character:** Institutional heritage, authentic campus imagery, formal editorial organization.
- **Primary screens:** Audience pathways (prospective/current/staff/alumni), programmes, admissions, research, faculty, campus information.
- **Distinctive:** Department identities and program comparisons.
- **Avoid:** Generic corporate SaaS identity that loses academic character.

#### 23. Documentation/research portal — reading and relationships
- **Character:** Text-first, calm, accessible, technically accurate.
- **Primary screens:** Documentation hierarchy, search, in-page TOC, topic pages, examples, linked references, citations.
- **Distinctive:** Contextual cross-references, expandable examples, relation diagrams.
- **Avoid:** Cards around every paragraph, excessive width and needless animation.

### E. Communication and community

#### 24. Social media — content-centered
- **Character:** Understated UI so the content defines the experience.
- **Primary screens:** Feed, compose, discovery, user profiles, comments, notifications, settings.
- **Distinctive:** Composition and discovery tailored to actual content (photo/video/text).
- **Avoid:** Elaborate chrome that distracts from content and social interaction.

#### 25. Messaging — conversation-first
- **Character:** Calm, personal, readable, fast.
- **Primary screens:** Conversation list, message area, composer, attachments, search, thread/group details.
- **Distinctive:** Excellent message navigation, reactions, threads, contextual media.
- **Avoid:** Huge message cards, ornamental effects and slow routine interactions.

#### 26. Forums/discussion — community knowledge
- **Character:** Thoughtful, information-dense, readable.
- **Primary screens:** Categories, thread listings, conversation pages, accepted answers/moderation, search.
- **Distinctive:** Nested replies, quoting and subject/topic exploration.
- **Avoid:** Photo-first design when discussion is the core product.

#### 27. Dating/relationships — human and privacy-aware
- **Character:** Warm, welcoming, personal; not excessively gamified.
- **Primary screens:** Profiles, prompts/interests, discovery, preferences, matches, messaging, safety settings.
- **Distinctive:** Meaningful profile prompts and respectful exploration.
- **Avoid:** Manipulative UI, intrusive animation, obscured privacy controls.

### F. Personal, lifestyle and public services

#### 28. Healthcare/medical — clinical clarity
- **Character:** Reassuring, high-trust, exceptionally legible.
- **Primary screens:** Appointments, medical records, prescriptions, practitioner communication, privacy controls.
- **Distinctive:** Medical timelines and clear instructions/preparation.
- **Avoid:** Playful decoration undermining clarity, charts without clinical context.

#### 29. Fitness/activity tracking — performance-led
- **Character:** Athletic, motivating, bold numbers, appropriate contrast.
- **Primary screens:** Today's activity, recording, goals, training plan, history, maps, splits, charts.
- **Distinctive:** Route maps, pace/elevation charts, satisfying milestones.
- **Avoid:** Inspirational quotes and filler cards replacing useful performance data.

#### 30. Personal productivity — calm focus
- **Character:** Restrained, paper-inspired or neutral, efficient.
- **Primary screens:** Today view, quick capture, projects, calendar, focus mode, task detail.
- **Distinctive:** Daily timeline and intentional completion feedback.
- **Avoid:** Excessive gamification or multiple redundant task representations.

#### 31. Personal budgeting/expense tracking — practical clarity
- **Character:** Balanced, straightforward and trustworthy.
- **Primary screens:** Available money, quick entry, recent transactions, categories, budgets, goals, reports.
- **Distinctive:** Financial calendar and useful spending comparison.
- **Avoid:** Heavy decorative analytics without decisions or practical value.

#### 32. Government/civic — public-service accessibility
- **Character:** Clear, institutional and dependable.
- **Primary screens:** Service directory, requirements, applications, document uploads, processing progress, confirmation/help.
- **Distinctive:** Visible step-by-step eligibility and application journey.
- **Avoid:** SaaS-marketing flourishes, inaccessible forms, unnecessary account barriers.

#### 33. Nonprofit/charity — mission and evidence
- **Character:** Human, credible, compassionate, honest.
- **Primary screens:** Mission, programs, impact evidence, stories, contribute, volunteer, transparent reporting.
- **Distinctive:** Authentic documentary storytelling with verifiable outcomes.
- **Avoid:** Generic emotional stock imagery and unsupported impact claims.

### G. Technical and specialized

#### 34. Logistics/fleet — operations command center
- **Character:** High-information operational precision.
- **Primary screens:** Live/near-real-time map, vehicle list, dispatch, shipments, alerts, route details, incidents.
- **Distinctive:** Synchronized maps/tables and operational timelines.
- **Avoid:** Large low-information dashboards that delay decisions.

#### 35. Maps/navigation — geography first
- **Character:** Cartographic utility with minimal surrounding controls.
- **Primary screens:** Map, search, place detail, route planning, guidance, saved locations.
- **Distinctive:** Context-aware floating tools and expandable bottom sheets.
- **Avoid:** Permanent bulky panels hiding the map.

#### 36. Creative editors — canvas-first professional workspace
- **Character:** Neutral tools, large viewport, compact precision controls.
- **Primary screens:** Canvas, toolbars, layers/objects, inspector, assets, history, export.
- **Distinctive:** Customizable panels, precision inputs and keyboard shortcuts.
- **Avoid:** Giant cards consuming the work area.

#### 37. Cybersecurity/monitoring — analytical risk interface
- **Character:** Technical, serious, event-focused; status colors have meaning.
- **Primary screens:** Alerts, severity, impacted assets, event timeline, evidence, incidents, investigation tools.
- **Distinctive:** Correlated events and actionable investigation context.
- **Avoid:** Fake hacker screens, gratuitous terminal animation and unreadable neon.

#### 38. AI tools — task-led and transparent
- **Character:** Adapt to the work: coding tool resembles an IDE, writing tool resembles an editor.
- **Primary screens:** Main input/task, configurable runs, results, editable output, history, sources/errors where relevant.
- **Distinctive:** Comparisons, reviewable edits and understandable model limitations.
- **Avoid:** Glowing AI orbs and chat interfaces added where they don't improve the task.

#### 39. Automotive/transport services — engineered precision
- **Character:** Industrial, premium, technical, vehicle-oriented.
- **Primary screens:** Vehicles, health/diagnostics, maintenance, bookings, service history, costs.
- **Distinctive:** Vehicle diagrams, maintenance timelines and technical detail.
- **Avoid:** Generic admin layout that ignores physical vehicles and their workflows.

#### 40. Utility/device management — native simplicity
- **Character:** Fast, lightweight, platform-consistent.
- **Primary screens:** Primary action, relevant state, settings, permissions, history and help.
- **Distinctive:** Native-feeling controls, keyboard shortcuts, correct OS integrations.
- **Avoid:** Web-marketing sections or oversized cards inside a simple utility.

---

### Visual design QA checklist

- [ ] Brand-specific typography and visual identity, not a copied template.
- [ ] Content hierarchy uses different compositions where appropriate.
- [ ] Imagery belongs to this product and is licensed/owned.
- [ ] Realistic data and content lengths are used in mockups/tests.
- [ ] Core functions work, persist, and connect to other relevant features.
- [ ] Motion is justified; reduced-motion settings respected.
- [ ] Information density matches user goals.
- [ ] Components are cohesive yet not mechanically repetitive.
- [ ] Tablet/mobile layouts are intentionally adapted.
- [ ] Accessibility, keyboard controls, focus, contrast, and error messaging reviewed.
